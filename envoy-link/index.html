Envoy Link

Internal use statement, covering both the end-user license agreement and the privacy policy. Last updated October 30, 2026.

What this is

Envoy Link is a private tool used only by the owners and team of Envoy, Bevel LLC, Cover LLC and Kroenung LLC. It connects each company's own QuickBooks Online account to the company's internal reporting spreadsheet. It is not offered to the public, to customers or to any other business.

Use

Only authorized members of these companies may use Envoy Link, and only with those companies' own QuickBooks data. No license is granted to anyone else.

Data we handle

Envoy Link reads accounting data (invoices, bills, balances and reports) from the connected QuickBooks companies and writes it to a Google Sheet owned by the company. It does not collect personal information from the public.

Sharing and storage

The data is not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party. It is stored only in the company's own Google Workspace and in the authorization tokens held by the tool. Access is limited to authorized team members.

Disconnecting

The companies can disconnect Envoy Link at any time from QuickBooks (My Apps) or by removing the stored authorization, which stops all access immediately.
